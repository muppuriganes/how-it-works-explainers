So let's put it all together. Your voice becomes numbers. The numbers become radio waves, flying to the nearest tower. The network finds your friend's phone and makes it ring. Your packets race through fibre, and under the ocean. Then back into the air, and back into sound. And all of this happens fifty times every second, in both directions, for the whole call. So the next time you say hello to someone far away, remember: your voice just travelled across the world, and arrived in about a quarter of a second.
