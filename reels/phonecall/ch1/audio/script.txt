You open your phone, tap a name, and press call. A few seconds later, a phone rings on the other side of the planet. And when your friend says hello, their voice reaches you in about a quarter of a second. In that tiny moment, your voice turns into numbers, flies through the air as invisible waves, races through glass cables under the ocean, and turns back into sound. Let's follow one hello, step by step, from your mouth to their ear.
