And now, the scariest message in India. Money debited. Payment failed. What happened? Usually, your bank did take the money out, but somewhere after that, a confirmation got stuck. Maybe a server was slow. Maybe the network sneezed. So the system isn't sure the chai wala got paid. Don't panic. Your money isn't lost. It's just stuck in traffic. By the rules, it should come back to your account automatically by the next working day. And if the bank takes longer, the rules say it has to pay you a hundred rupees for every extra day. So technically, a slow bank could buy you ten chais.
