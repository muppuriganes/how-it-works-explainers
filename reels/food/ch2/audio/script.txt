The moment you pay, your order flies to a tablet in the restaurant kitchen. It beeps. Loudly. Until someone taps accept. Now the app has to make a guess: how long will this biryani take? That guess is called food preparation time, and a machine learning model makes it. It looks at what you ordered, how many items, and how busy that kitchen is right now. One biryani on a Tuesday afternoon? Quick. Forty orders on a Friday night? Good luck. And this guess matters more than anything. Because it decides exactly when to send your rider.
